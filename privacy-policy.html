Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026

This policy explains how the DataFort - Data Restore Helper app for Android (package name com.datario.virexa.datafort, "DataFort", "we", "us") handles information.

What the app accesses on your device

  • Files and storage: DataFort accesses your device storage only to scan for files that may be recoverable and to save the files you select back to your device. This happens on your device. We do not upload your files, their contents or their names.
  • Notifications: DataFort may show a progress or completion notification while a scan or restore is running.

We do not run user accounts, and we do not collect your files or scan results on any server of our own.

Ads

DataFort is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising ID.
  • Device information, such as device model, operating system version and language.
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • Ad interactions, such as views and taps.

Learn how Google uses this information in How Google uses information in advertising. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or limit ad personalization, in your phone's settings.

Consent (GDPR and UK)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or another region where consent is required, DataFort uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can review or change your choice at any time in the app under Settings > Privacy settings.

Children

DataFort is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data retention and deletion

Files you scan and restore stay on your device. Uninstalling DataFort, or clearing its data in your phone's settings, removes the app's local data. Restored files that you saved to your device remain until you delete them. Data collected by Google for ads is kept according to Google's policies.

Security

Because scanning and restoring happen on your device and your files are not uploaded by us, your files stay protected by your device's own security. Data sent to Google for ads is handled by Google's ads services.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date at the top of this page shows when the current version took effect.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy or your data, please contact us using the developer contact details shown on DataFort's Google Play Store page.
