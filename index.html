Deleted a photo, video or document by mistake? DataFort helps you look for files on your own phone that may still be recoverable, and restore the ones you choose.

Get it on Google Play

What DataFort does

Scan your storage

Start a scan to look for photos, videos, audio, documents and other files that may still be recoverable.

Choose a file type

Pick what to look for, such as photos, videos, audio or documents, so the results stay focused.

Review before restoring

See what the scan found before anything is saved. Nothing is restored automatically.

Restore what you select

Select the items you need and save them back to your phone.

Progress and alerts

Follow progress while a task runs, and get a notification when it finishes if you leave the app.

Stays on your phone

Scanning and restoring happen on your device. Your files are not uploaded for recovery.

What to expect

Results depend on your storage, how long ago a file was deleted, how your device handles deleted data, and whether that data has already been overwritten. No recovery app can bring back every deleted file, so regular backups are still the safest way to protect what matters.
